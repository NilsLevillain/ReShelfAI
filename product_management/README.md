# Reshelf: Product Management

How Reshelf was framed as a product before it was built. Start here.

| Folder | Question it answers | Status |
|---|---|---|
| `discovery/` | What do we know, what are we assuming, how do we test it? | Done (round 1) |
| `metrics/` | How do we know it works? North Star and input metrics | Done (draft) |
| `product_statement/` | Who is it for, what problem, what vision? | To write |
| `differentiators/` | Why Reshelf and not a spreadsheet or a planning suite? | To write |

## Read in 5 minutes

1. `discovery/discovery_plan.md`: the riskiest assumptions and the experiments that test them.
2. `discovery/feature_triage.md`: what goes in the MVP and what does not, with reasons.
3. `metrics/metrics_dashboard.md`: the North Star and the metrics tree.

## Ground rules

* Clean room: all data is synthetic, every number is a hypothesis or an illustrative default, and no figure here claims real business impact.
* Evidence levels are stated. Until interviews run, priorities are hypotheses.
