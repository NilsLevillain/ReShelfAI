## Read in 5 minutes

1. `1_discovery/1_discovery_plan.md`: the riskiest assumptions and the experiments that test them.
2. `1_discovery/3_feature_triage.md`: what goes in the MVP and what does not, with reasons.
3. `2_strategy/1_strategy.md`: vision, segments, trade-offs, defensibility.
4. `2_strategy/5_pricing.md`: how a first revenue test would work.

## Ground rules

* Clean room: all data is synthetic, every number is a hypothesis or an illustrative default, and no figure here claims real business impact.
* Evidence levels are stated. Until interviews run, priorities are hypotheses.
* Claims about competitors and regulation are tagged verified, background knowledge or hypothesis. Verify before quoting publicly.
