## Method

* Personas span the segment boundary on purpose (red team K3): two beauty planners, two other dated goods, two reverse logistics, two sustainability or finance reviewers.
* Answers are written to include disagreement and a "no", because an all yes sample says nothing.

## What the simulation suggests testing first in real calls

1. **Who owns the budget.** The simulated answers split by segment: beauty planners and reverse logistics do not name an owner, sustainability does. Ask this early, past behavior form ("who signed off the last time you spent money on this?").
2. **Does a legacy tool already cover it (S4).** If a real planner says yes, the planner productivity angle weakens.
3. **Final call sits with a returns team (S3, S5).** Test whether the primary persona is a planner at all.
4. **Do finance and sustainability want the euro cost of a rule (S7 no, S8 yes).** This is the K1 kill criterion for the Why panel.

## Leading question check on the script

* "Tell me about the last time you decided where to send near expiry stock" is a past behavior question and stays.
* Any question that names a rule cost, a Why panel or an optimizer is a pitch. Move those to E3.
* Add a neutral opener for reverse logistics and finance personas, who do not "decide where to send" stock.
